# Leverage Trading

## Platforms

- **Hyperliquid** — primary L1 DEX, crypto perps
- **Avantis** — perpetuals on Base

## Limits

| Asset class | Max leverage |
|-------------|-------------|
| Crypto | 50x |
| Forex | 100x |
| Commodities | 100x |

Supported assets: BTC, ETH, SOL, and many more crypto; EUR/USD, GBP/USD, etc. forex; gold, silver, oil, natural gas commodities.

## Risk

> "Leverage amplifies both gains and losses."

Total collateral loss is possible. Not suitable for beginners without proper risk controls.

## Essential Risk Rules

1. **Always use stop-loss** — non-negotiable
2. **Position size**: 1–5% of capital per trade
3. **Know your liquidation price** before opening
4. **Test small** before scaling up
5. **Monitor funding rates** (charged every 8h on Hyperliquid)
