# Market Research

Research tokens and analyze market data using Bankr's AI-powered analysis.

## Capabilities

- Token search across chains
- Price and market data
- Technical analysis (RSI, MACD, MAs, Bollinger Bands)
- Social sentiment analysis
- Price charts
- Trending tokens
- Token comparisons

## Prompt Examples

```
"What's the price of ETH?"
"Show me ETH market data"
"Do technical analysis on ETH"
"What's the sentiment on ETH?"
"Show me ETH price chart"
"What tokens are trending?"
"Compare ETH vs SOL"
"New tokens on Base"
"Top gainers today"
```

## Data Available

- Current USD price, 24h/7d/30d change, ATH/ATL
- Market cap, FDV, 24h volume, supply metrics, holder count
- Technical indicators: RSI, MACD, MAs, support/resistance, Bollinger Bands
- Social: Twitter mentions, sentiment, social volume, influencer activity

## Supported Chains

Base, Polygon, Ethereum, Solana, Unichain

## Limitations

- Historical data limited to available timeframes
- New tokens may have limited data
- Analysis is informational, not investment advice
